Our Services

Four Services, One Standard of Excellence

Interior Painting

Living rooms, bedrooms, kitchens, hallways, trim and ceilings — finished with careful prep, clean lines and paints chosen to suit how each room is actually used.

  • Full surface preparation & patching
  • Walls, ceilings, woodwork & trim
  • Color consultation on request
  • Dust-conscious, tidy working practice

Exterior Painting

Weatherproof finishes for siding, stucco, wood and trim that stand up to the Midwest climate — protecting your property through hard winters and humid summers alike.

  • Power washing & surface repair
  • Siding, stucco, fascia & soffits
  • Weather-appropriate scheduling
  • Durable, weatherproof paint systems

Staining & Wallcoverings

Stain and sealant work that lets the grain show through, plus careful wallpaper hanging and removal — the finishes that ask for the steadiest hand.

  • Decks, fences & exterior woodwork
  • Interior trim, doors & cabinetry
  • Wallpaper hanging & removal
  • Accent and feature walls

Power Washing

A thorough wash strips off the grime, chalking and mildew that stop paint bonding — worth doing on its own, and essential before any exterior repaint.

  • Siding, brick & stucco
  • Decks, patios & walkways
  • Driveways & fencing
  • Pressure matched to the surface
